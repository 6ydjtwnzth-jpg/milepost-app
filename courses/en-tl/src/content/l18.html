Tinanong ng bagong kadeteng si Tom si Ravi kung nakaranas na siya ng bagyo sa dagat. Ikinuwento ni Ravi ang isang gabi noong nakaraang taon. Sa araling ito, matututunan mo ang {past continuous} ({I was sleeping}), ang pagsasama nito sa {past simple} ({when the alarm rang}), at ang {while}, {suddenly}, {in the end}.

Sabi ni Ravi, {I was sleeping when the alarm rang}. Dalawang nakaraan sa isang pangungusap! Alin ang nagaganap at alin ang biglang nangyari?

{I was sleeping} = «natutulog ako» (nagaganap, {past continuous}). {the alarm rang} = «tumunog ang alarma» (pangyayari, {past simple}). Isipin ang pelikula: ang {past continuous} ang tanawin, ang {past simple} ang aksyon.

{past continuous}: {was/were} + {-ing}

PositiboNegatiboTanong
I / he / she / itI was sleeping.I wasn't sleeping.Was she sleeping?
you / we / theyWe were sailing.We weren't sailing.Were they sailing?

Gawaing nagaganap sa isang sandali sa nakaraan: {At midnight I was working}. Sa kuwento, para sa tagpo: {It was raining}, {The ship was moving}.

{when} at {while}

{when} + pangyayari ({past simple}): {I was sleeping when the alarm rang}. {while} + nagaganap ({past continuous}): {While we were waiting, we helped the passengers}.
